1. Who is responsible?

Springy Zingy is developed and maintained by Damian Cichosz, an individual developer based in Poland. For privacy questions about the game or requests concerning processing for which I am responsible, contact chatoyment@wp.pl.

For some operations, third-party providers such as Google, Monetag or Telegram determine their own purposes and means of processing and may act as separate controllers or otherwise under their own terms. Please also review their privacy notices linked in this policy.

2. Platforms covered by this policy

This policy covers:

  • Android: the Springy Zingy mobile game, including Google Play Games Services for player sign-in and leaderboards and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising.
  • Telegram Mini App / WebGL: the browser-based version of Springy Zingy launched through Telegram, which uses the Telegram Web Apps interface and Monetag advertising technology.
  • This policy website: this privacy policy page is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when you visit the page under its own policies.

The game does not currently provide a separate developer-operated account system or a central player-profile database. This statement does not apply to the services and infrastructure operated by third-party providers.

3. Information that may be processed

The information involved depends on your platform, device settings, the features you use, the advertising configuration and the choices available to you. I do not ask players to provide a phone number, postal address, payment-card details or password directly to Springy Zingy.

Game progress and settings

The game may store basic progress and preferences locally on your device or, in the WebGL version, in browser storage. This can include game settings, best-score information and in-game progress such as Flames. Local data may be removed if you clear the app's data, uninstall the app, clear browser storage or use the relevant device controls.

Google Play Games Services

On Android, when Google Play Games Services is used, Google may process a player's Play Games identifier, player name or public profile information and scores submitted to leaderboards. These features are used to sign the player in and submit or display leaderboard results. Springy Zingy does not need the player's Google account password to use these features.

Advertising and technical information

Advertising SDKs and providers may automatically process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, device model, operating system and app or browser information, general location inferred from IP address, ad impressions and interactions, frequency-cap information, and diagnostic or performance data. The exact information depends on the provider, device, consent choices and configuration.

Telegram Mini App information

Telegram operates the platform through which the web version is launched. Telegram may process account, device, connection and usage information under its own privacy policy. Telegram's Mini Apps interface can make certain user context available to a Mini App. The current game is not designed to use a developer-operated server to build a Telegram user profile; this may change if future versions add features that process additional Telegram data.

Information you send directly

If you email me, I will receive the information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address and the contents of your request. I use it to respond and handle the request.

4. Why information is processed

Depending on the feature and platform, information may be processed to:

  • run the game and keep local game progress and preferences;
  • authenticate a player through Google Play Games Services and submit leaderboard scores;
  • request, display and measure ads, including rewarded ads and ad frequency controls;
  • prevent invalid ad activity, fraud, abuse and technical problems;
  • diagnose crashes and maintain service reliability where relevant;
  • remember and respect applicable privacy and advertising choices; and
  • respond to messages sent to the developer.

Where the GDPR applies, the relevant legal basis depends on the processing. It may include providing a feature you request, legitimate interests such as maintaining and securing the game where those interests are not overridden by your rights, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required, including for certain advertising or storage technologies. Third-party providers may determine their own legal bases for processing they carry out.

6. Advertising, consent and privacy choices

Springy Zingy displays ads in order to support development. The Android version uses AdMob; the Telegram Mini App / WebGL version uses Monetag. Some ads may be personalized and others may be non-personalized or limited, depending on the provider, the user's location, available privacy signals, device settings and the consent choices that apply.

On Android, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tools may be used to request and update advertising privacy choices where required. If the game shows a Privacy Settings option in its Settings screen, it can be used to reopen the available privacy options. Users can also review advertising ID controls in their Android device settings.

For the Telegram Mini App, ad-related technologies and choices depend on the Monetag integration, browser and platform features. This policy page does not itself set advertising cookies, load ad tags or collect advertising identifiers.

Choosing not to consent to personalized ads does not necessarily mean that no ads will be shown. Depending on the applicable rules and configuration, non-personalized or limited ads may still be available. Consent settings should be managed through the in-game or provider controls presented to you.

Google explains its advertising data practices here: Google's business data responsibility and privacy information.

7. Cookies, local storage and retention

The native Android version may use local application storage for game progress and settings. The WebGL version may use browser storage, including storage mechanisms provided by Unity and the browser, to retain local game data. Advertising providers may use cookies, local storage, mobile advertising identifiers, SDKs or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention or other purposes described in their policies.

I do not control the retention periods used by Google, Monetag, Telegram, GitHub or other third-party providers. Their own privacy notices explain their retention practices and any available deletion or opt-out processes.

Local game data normally remains on your device or in browser storage until it is overwritten or removed by the game, the browser, the operating system or you. Email correspondence is retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and keep appropriate records.

8. Sharing and international transfers

I do not operate a separate server or database for storing players' game profiles in the current version. Information may nevertheless be transmitted directly between your device and the third-party services needed for leaderboards, advertising, hosting, security and related functions. These providers may use affiliates, service providers and advertising partners as described in their own policies.

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, those providers are responsible for describing the safeguards and legal mechanisms they use for international transfers in their privacy documentation.

I do not directly sell players' personal information. Advertising providers may process information for their own services and purposes under their terms, subject to applicable law and the choices and controls available to you.

9. Your privacy rights

If the GDPR applies to you, you may have the right, subject to legal conditions, to request access to personal data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before the withdrawal.

To exercise rights relating to processing for which I am responsible, contact chatoyment@wp.pl. Requests relating to data held by Google, Monetag, Telegram or GitHub may need to be sent to the relevant provider using its own privacy tools or contact channels.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO): uodo.gov.pl.

10. Children's privacy

Springy Zingy is not specifically designed for children under 13, and I do not knowingly ask children under 13 to submit personal information directly to me. The game and its third-party services may still be accessible to users of different ages. Advertising and privacy settings must be configured according to the audience and applicable legal requirements.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent personal information directly to me, contact me at chatoyment@wp.pl. I will review the request and take appropriate steps within my control. Data held by third-party providers may need to be addressed with those providers directly.

11. Security and changes to this policy

Reasonable steps are taken to keep the game and this policy page maintained. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security. Third-party services apply their own security measures, as described in their policies.

This policy may be updated when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with its effective date updated above. Please review this page from time to time.
